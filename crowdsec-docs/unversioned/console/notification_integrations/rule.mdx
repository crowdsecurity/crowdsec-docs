Notification rules allow you to customize the alerts and notifications you receive from your CrowdSec Console.
By setting up specific rules, you can ensure that you are only notified about events that are relevant to your organization.
This guide will walk you through the process of creating a notification rule for your linked integration.

> Every organization can send notifications by [email](#email-destination) without linking anything.
> To send them to Discord, Slack or a webhook, link the integration first: see the [Integrations Overview](overview.mdx).

## Create a notification rule

1. In the [CrowdSec Console](https://app.crowdsec.net), navigate to **Notification Rules** and click on **Add Rule**.

## Email destination

The Email destination is available in every organization, with nothing to link or configure. It sends the rule's notifications by email to the members you select.

### Recipients

- Recipients are always members of your organization: you pick them from a list, and cannot type an address. To notify someone else, invite them to your organization first: the **Invite a member** link below the recipients opens your organization's members settings.
- An Email destination needs at least one recipient, and accepts up to 10, or fewer if your organization has fewer member seats.

### Events not sent by email

Some events cannot be sent to an Email destination. In the event list, they carry a crossed-out envelope icon, and hovering it gives the reason:

- **Already emailed by the Console**: Am I Under Attack, the alert retention quota events, Payment Failed, Security Engine Enrolled and Security Engine Unenrolled. The Console already emails them to your members, and each member chooses whether to receive them in their own notification settings.
- **Too frequent**: Alert Triggered fires on every alert, which is too often for email. Send it to Discord, Slack or a webhook instead.

A rule cannot combine one of these events with an Email destination. If you select both, a warning explains why and the rule cannot be saved until you remove the event or the destination. In the Threat Hunting category, where a rule has a single event, Email is not offered as a destination for these events.
