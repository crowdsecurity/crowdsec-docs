## What Premium helps you achieve

CrowdSec Premium extends your organization with advanced protection, team collaboration, operational automation, and deeper threat intelligence. It applies at the organization level — all Security Engines in the organization share its entitlements.

<div className="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 mb-8">

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### Improve proactive protection

Block attackers before they reach your infrastructure. Premium expands the Community Blocklist from 3k to 50k IPs, generates an organization-specific Threat Forecast from your own shared signals, and removes scanner noise from your alert view.

[Feature reference →](/u/console/premium_upgrade/features_overview)

</div>

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### Centralize security management

Manage decisions, allowlists, and blocklist subscriptions from one place and propagate them to every Security Engine and integration automatically. No manual per-engine configuration.

[Remediation Sync →](/u/console/remediation_sync)

</div>

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### Investigate and monitor at scale

Retain up to one year of alerts, investigate IP reputation directly in the Console, and receive real-time notifications when attack patterns change or engines go offline.

[Alerts and retention →](/u/console/alerts/quotas)

</div>

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### Automate multi-environment operations

Use the Service API to manage enrollments, decisions, and custom blocklists programmatically. Deploy Security Engines at scale with Auto Enroll and infrastructure-as-code tooling.

[Service API →](/u/console/service_api/getting_started)

</div>

</div>

## Is Premium appropriate for you?

<div className="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-4 mb-8">

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### DevOps / small infrastructure

You manage a handful of servers or containers and want better blocking coverage with less alert noise and no manual remediation work.

Key features: expanded blocklist, Threat Forecast, Background Noise filtering, Remediation Sync, Auto Enroll.

</div>

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### Security operations team

Your team investigates incidents, needs historical data for audits, and must collaborate without stepping on each other in a shared console.

Key features: 5 seats, 365-day retention, IP investigation quotas, CTI API, push notifications, centralized allowlists.

</div>

<div className="border border-solid border-border rounded-lg p-6 bg-card">

### MSP / multi-tenant environment

You manage security for multiple clients or isolated environments and need complete data separation, API-driven automation, and the ability to share custom threat intelligence.

Key features: unlimited organizations, Service API, blocklist creation and sharing, Auto Enroll.

</div>

</div>

## Community vs Premium

| Entitlement | Community | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Community Blocklist | Top 3k IPs | Top 50k IPs |
| Blocklist subscriptions | 3 | Unlimited |
| Alert quota | 500 / month | 10k per Security Engine / month |
| Alert retention | 60 days | 365 days |
| Organization seats | 1 | 5 included |
| Console IP investigations | 30 / week | 100 / week |
| CTI API calls | 120 / month | 1,500 / month |
| Remediation Sync | — | Yes |
| Background Noise filtering | — | Yes |
| Am I Under Attack | — | Yes |
| Threat Forecast Blocklist | — | Yes |
| Service API | — | Yes |
| Multiple organizations | — | Yes |

For full entitlement details and expandable limits, see [Quotas and plan limits](/u/console/premium_upgrade/quotas_limits).
