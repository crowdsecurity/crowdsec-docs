If you keep re-applying the same filters every morning, the Explorer can do it for you. This page shows you how to turn any combination of filters, period and layout into a named view, pin it to the navigation menu, and share it with your team. By the end, your Alerts page opens directly on what you monitor.

## Save what you are looking at

Set up the Explorer the way you want it - filters, period, breakdowns, grouping. As soon as your setup differs from the current view, a **Save** action appears in the views bar. Name the view; it now belongs to your organization, and anyone in it can use it.

[capture: views bar with the save action visible on a modified state]

## Pin it to the menu

Pin a view and it appears as a sub-entry of the Alerts menu item, one click from anywhere in the Console. An organization can pin up to 3 views - keep them for the perspectives your team opens daily.

[capture: sidebar menu with pinned views under Alerts Explorer]

## Work with views

- **Update** - change the filters while a view is active, then save the changes into it, or save them as a new view.
- **Duplicate** - start a new view from an existing one.
- **Share** - views live in the URL like everything else: send the link, the recipient lands on the same view.

The built-in views (All, WAF, Live map) are always there and cannot be edited - your custom views live alongside them.

## Key considerations

- Views are shared at the organization level: renaming or deleting one affects your whole team.
- An organization can keep up to 3 custom views; the Console tells you when you hit the limit.
