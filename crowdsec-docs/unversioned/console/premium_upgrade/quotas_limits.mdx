This page is the single source of truth for quantitative plan limits. All other Premium documentation references these values rather than repeating them.

:::info
Entitlement values here reflect the standard plan tiers. Custom volumes are available for enterprise and MSP arrangements — contact [the CrowdSec team](https://www.crowdsec.net/contact-crowdsec) for details.
:::

---

## Protection limits

| Entitlement | Community | Premium (base) | Expandable | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Community Blocklist size | 3,000 IPs | 50,000 IPs | No | IPs per organization |
| Blocklist subscriptions | 3 | Unlimited | N/A | Per organization |
| Threat Forecast Blocklist | Not available | Included | N/A | Per organization |
| Premium tier blocklist access | Not available | Included | N/A | Per organization |

---

## Alert limits

| Entitlement | Community | Premium (base) | Expandable | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alert quota | 500 | 10,000 | Yes | Per organization per month |
| Alert retention | 60 days | 365 days | No | Per organization |

:::note
The Premium alert quota of 10,000 applies per Security Engine per month by default. Organizations with many engines should verify their expected volume. Contact CrowdSec if you need a custom quota.
:::

---

## Investigation limits

These are two distinct entitlements. Console IP investigations are performed through the Console interface. CTI API calls are made programmatically via the API.

| Entitlement | Community | Premium (base) | Expandable | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Console IP investigations | 30 | 100 | Yes | Per organization per week |
| CTI API calls | 120 | 1,500 | Yes | Per API key per month |

## Organization and team limits

| Entitlement | Community | Premium (base) | Expandable | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organization seats | 1 | 5 | Yes | Per organization |
| Additional organizations | 1 (free) | Unlimited | N/A | Per account |

## Support entitlements

| Entitlement | Community | Premium | Advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support channel | Community forum / Discord | Console chat | Console chat |
| First contact SLA | No SLA | 3 business days | 1 business day |
| Workaround / solution SLA | No SLA | 5 business days | 3 business days |
| Support hours | N/A | 8 AM–5 PM CET, business days | 8 AM–5 PM CET + out-of-hours for critical |
| Scope | Community | Console platform | Console + Engines + Integrations + all products |

---

## Notes on expandable limits

Entitlements marked as expandable can be increased beyond the base plan values. Contact [CrowdSec](https://www.crowdsec.net/contact-crowdsec) to discuss:

- Additional organization seats beyond 5
- Higher Console IP investigation quotas
- Higher CTI API call quotas
- Custom alert volumes for large-scale deployments
